Basavarajappa

I work at the intersection of condensed matter theory and materials design — using first-principles DFT to unravel the electronic, optical, and vibrational behaviour of perovskites and van der Waals heterostructures under pressure, strain, and external fields. My goal: accelerating the discovery of materials for next-generation photovoltaics and optoelectronics.
